Complete QR-Based Restaurant Management & Ordering System

Project Overview

QR Resto is a full-stack, QR-based restaurant ordering and management platform designed to digitize restaurant operations and create a seamless, contactless dining experience. It allows customers to scan table-specific QR codes, browse digital menus, customize dishes, and place orders directly from their smartphones without installing any application.

Beyond customer ordering, the platform provides a comprehensive restaurant administration system where restaurant owners and staff can manage menus, categories, tables, orders, billing, employees, and daily operations through a centralized dashboard.

The system supports multiple restaurants with organization-level management and role-based access control, making it scalable for individual restaurants as well as multi-restaurant businesses.

Key Features

1. QR-Based Ordering System

  • Unique QR codes for individual restaurant tables
  • Automatic table identification when customers scan QR codes
  • Direct access to restaurant-specific digital menus
  • No application installation or customer registration required
  • Seamless mobile-first ordering experience

2. Digital Menu Management

  • Dynamic menu with food images, descriptions, and pricing
  • Category and subcategory management
  • Menu item availability and customization
  • Dynamic menu updates through the admin panel
  • Organized food listings for an intuitive customer experience

3. Customer Ordering Experience

  • Interactive shopping cart with quantity modification
  • Special instructions and item customization
  • Table-specific order placement
  • Order confirmation and status tracking
  • Real-time order status updates

4. Restaurant Admin Dashboard

  • Centralized dashboard for restaurant operations
  • Manage orders, menus, categories, and subcategories
  • Table and restaurant management
  • Daily revenue and order tracking
  • Payment management, discounts, and billing
  • Order cancellation and status management

5. Kitchen Order Ticket (KOT) & Thermal Printing

  • Dedicated KOT generation for incoming orders
  • One-click KOT printing from the order dashboard
  • Compact 58mm thermal receipt format
  • Includes restaurant name, date, time, table number, order number, item names, and quantities
  • Separate customer bill printing functionality

6. Staff & Role-Based Management

  • Multiple staff roles: Owner, Manager, Receptionist, and Restaurant Admin
  • Role-based permissions and access restrictions
  • Manager and receptionist assignment to restaurants
  • Staff capacity limits based on subscription plans
  • Controlled access to restaurant operations

7. Notifications & Order Management

  • Notifications for incoming orders
  • Audio notification alerts for new orders
  • Order status management with Ready and other operational stages
  • Improved coordination between restaurant staff and kitchen operations

8. Organization & Multi-Restaurant Management

  • Organization-level restaurant management
  • Support for multiple restaurants under an organization
  • Subscription-based plans and staff limits
  • Centralized ownership and administration

Technical Implementation

The platform uses a modular web architecture consisting of a customer-facing ordering application and a separate restaurant administration panel.

  • Frontend: JavaScript, React.js, HTML5, CSS3, responsive UI components
  • Build Tool: Vite
  • Backend & Database: Firebase Cloud Firestore for real-time data storage and synchronization
  • Authentication: Firebase Authentication for secure user authentication
  • Database Structure: Organized collections for organizations, restaurants, users, menus, and orders
  • QR Integration: QR code generation and table-specific routing
  • Browser Storage: LocalStorage for cart persistence and session-related data
  • Printing: Browser-based print functionality with custom CSS for 58mm thermal receipts and KOT tickets
  • Notifications: Browser-based audio notifications for incoming orders
  • Deployment: Vercel

Problem Solved

Traditional restaurant ordering often involves physical menus, manual order-taking, communication gaps between service staff and kitchen teams, and time-consuming billing processes.

QR Resto addresses these challenges by bringing the entire dining workflow into a single digital platform. Customers can independently browse menus and place orders, while restaurant staff can monitor incoming orders, manage operations, generate kitchen tickets, and process bills from a centralized dashboard.

This reduces manual intervention, improves order accuracy, streamlines kitchen communication, and provides restaurants with a more efficient way to manage daily operations.

Project Impact

QR Resto transforms traditional restaurant workflows into a connected digital ecosystem. By combining customer ordering, restaurant administration, staff management, real-time order handling, and thermal printing, it provides an end-to-end solution for modern restaurant operations.

The system significantly improved customer experience by reducing wait times and providing a modern, contactless ordering solution. Restaurants using QR Resto reported increased order accuracy, faster table turnover, enhanced kitchen coordination, and improved overall operational efficiency.
