Project Overview

Posteroid is a modern, full-stack eCommerce platform specializing in premium posters and photo frames. It provides customers with an intuitive online shopping experience where they can explore curated collections of automotive, sports, Formula 1, anime, and other visually appealing poster designs, customize their selections, and place orders through a seamless checkout process.

Built with Next.js and Firebase, Posteroid features a complete eCommerce ecosystem consisting of a customer-facing storefront, a secure backend, and a dedicated admin dashboard. The platform enables customers to browse products, manage their shopping carts, apply discount coupons, save shipping addresses, make online payments, and track their order status.

The project is designed to provide a scalable foundation for managing an online poster and photo frame business, from product management and order processing to shipping and content management.

Key Features

Customer-Facing Storefront

  • Product Catalogue: Browse a wide range of posters and photo frames across categories such as cars, sports, Formula 1, bikes, anime, and more
  • Product Details: Dedicated product pages with high-quality images, descriptions, pricing, available variants, and product information
  • Category Navigation: Explore products through organized categories and collections
  • Shopping Cart: Add products, update quantities, remove items, and view automatically calculated order summary
  • Coupon System: Apply percentage-based, fixed-amount, and free-delivery coupons during checkout
  • Address Management: Save, edit, and manage multiple shipping addresses with default address option
  • Secure Checkout: Complete orders through integrated checkout flow with server-side order validation
  • Online Payments: Razorpay integration for secure online payment processing
  • Order Management: View order history, order details, payment status, and shipping status through customer account
  • Responsive Design: Fully responsive shopping experience optimized for mobile phones, tablets, and desktop devices

Admin Dashboard

A dedicated admin panel provides centralized control over the entire eCommerce operation:

  • Product Management: Add, edit, update, and archive products with images, descriptions, categories, pricing, variants, and SEO information
  • Category Management: Organize and manage product categories
  • Order Management: View customer orders, manage order statuses, and monitor payment and shipping information
  • Coupon Management: Create and manage percentage, fixed-discount, and free-delivery coupons
  • Review Management: Manage customer product reviews
  • Banner Management: Create and manage promotional banners displayed on the storefront
  • Blog Management: Publish and manage blog posts with featured images, inline images, and formatted content
  • Custom Order Uploads: Manage customer-uploaded files associated with custom orders
  • Centralized Operations: Manage products, orders, promotions, and website content from one dashboard

Shipping & Order Fulfilment

  • Shipping Integration: Integration with Shiprocket and Delhivery for shipping operations
  • Courier Selection: Select the shipping provider for individual orders
  • Shipment Creation: Create shipments through selected courier provider after admin confirmation
  • Shipping Information: Store and manage courier details, AWB numbers, and tracking information
  • Shipping Charges: Automatically calculate shipping charges based on order subtotal, including free shipping eligibility

Technical Implementation

Posteroid is built using a modern full-stack architecture that separates the frontend, backend services, database, and external integrations.

The customer-facing application is developed using Next.js, providing a responsive and structured storefront with server-side API routes and optimized production builds.

The backend is powered by Firebase Cloud Functions, which handles business logic, API requests, order processing, payment verification, coupon validation, account operations, and shipping integrations.

Cloud Firestore is used as the primary NoSQL database to store and manage products, categories, customers, orders, coupons, reviews, banners, blog posts, carts, and website settings.

Firebase Authentication handles customer authentication, while Cloudinary is integrated for product images, promotional banners, blog media, and custom-order uploads.

For payments, the platform uses Razorpay, with server-side order creation and payment verification to maintain a secure checkout workflow. Shipping operations are integrated with Shiprocket and Delhivery. The frontend is deployed on Vercel, while backend services run on Firebase infrastructure.

Problem Solved

Many small and growing businesses need an online store that goes beyond simply displaying products. Managing product listings, customer orders, payments, discounts, shipping, and website content through disconnected tools can become complicated and time-consuming.

Posteroid addresses this by bringing the complete eCommerce workflow into one integrated platform. Customers get a convenient shopping experience with organized product collections, secure checkout, saved addresses, and order management. Administrators get a centralized dashboard to manage products, promotions, orders, content, and shipping operations without manually handling every process.

The platform reduces operational complexity, improves order management, and provides a scalable digital foundation for an online retail business.
